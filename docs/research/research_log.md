### Source 1: Agricultural Storage Research

**APA Reference:**  
Benimana, G. U., Ritho, C., & Irungu, P. (2021). Assessment of factors affecting the decision of smallholder farmers to use alternative maize storage technologies in Gatsibo District-Rwanda. *Heliyon, 7*(10), e08235.

**Type:** Research paper

**Link:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2021.e08235

**Key finding:** The study examines factors influencing smallholder farmers' decisions to adopt alternative maize storage technologies in Rwanda.

**Research gap:** The study focuses on storage technology adoption rather than integrating crop production, fertilizer use, productivity and post-harvest loss analysis into one database system.

### Source 2: Impact of Maize Storage Technologies

**APA Reference:**  
Benimana, G. U., Ritho, C., & Irungu, P. (2023). Impact of adopting maize hermetic storage technologies on smallholder farmers' income in Gatsibo District, Rwanda. *Heliyon, 9*(3), e14592.

**Type:** Research paper

**Link:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2023.e14592

**Key finding:** The research investigates the relationship between adopting hermetic maize storage technologies and smallholder farmers' income.

**Research gap:** It focuses on maize storage and income. The proposed project will extend the analysis to multiple crops, agricultural seasons, fertilizer-use patterns and potential marketable supply.

## Preliminary Research Conclusion

The reviewed sources demonstrate the importance of agricultural production information, fertilizer-use analysis, storage practices and food-loss assessment.

The proposed Rwanda Crop Production and Market Intelligence System will bring relevant agricultural information together in an Oracle database and use PL/SQL for calculations and business rules. Oracle APEX will provide an interactive interface for exploring production, fertilizer use, post-harvest losses and potential marketable supply.

**Data verification note:** Dataset availability, variable names, record counts, licensing conditions and relationships will be confirmed after obtaining and inspecting the underlying data files.
